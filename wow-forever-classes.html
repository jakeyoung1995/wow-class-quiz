Every class in WoW Forever: what changed, who can play it, how it levels

World of Warcraft: Forever is the game players spent years calling Classic Plus. Blizzard announced it on September 12, 2026, it is in beta now, and it launches on November 4, 2026. This page is the plain-English version of what is confirmed about its nine classes, sourced from Blizzard's own posts, Wowhead, Icy Veins and Warcraft Tavern, and re-checked as beta builds land.

Last checked September 26, 2026 against beta build 1.60.1. Anything Blizzard has not stated is marked as such. Racials and numbers can still change before launch.

What Forever changed for every class

Blizzard's Deep Dive panel and the September 24 beta development notes are the only official sources so far; Paladin is the only class with a full official writeup, and Blizzard says more class articles are coming. Across all nine classes, this much is confirmed:

  • Talent trees rebuilt. Same three trees per class, same rows, one-point milestone talents at 11, 21 and 31 plus a new one at 16. Blizzard's stated goal: "every single one of our 27 talent trees is a viable option to bring to your dungeon or raid group."
  • Buffs made baseline. Blessing of Kings, Divine Spirit, Improved Mark of the Wild and Improved Battle Shout no longer cost talent points.
  • Hit and crit merged. Spell, melee and ranged hit are one stat, and so is crit. Blizzard did this specifically to help Retribution Paladins and Enhancement Shaman.
  • Weapon skill defanged. Most weapon-skill racials became crit-chance racials, and Edgemaster's Handguards went from +7 to +1.
  • Healers can quest. Healing gear gives one third of its value as damage.
  • Combat pace. A solo mob is meant to take 10 to 15 seconds. Dungeon crowd control still matters, threat still matters, there is no level scaling and no flying.
  • Not announced: dual spec. The beta shows a locked Secondary tab and 1-silver respecs, but Blizzard calls the price temporary.

The Skyborne, briefly

Descendants of Highborne exiles who built a civilisation in Skywall. You pick a faction at creation: Windshapers join the Horde and can be Shaman, the High Order join the Alliance and can be Mages. Both get Warrior, Hunter, Rogue and Druid, with race-specific druid forms. Their starting zone is Zephras Isle, levels 1 to 12, reachable only by rolling a Skyborne. Racials: Walk on Air (a 10-second steerable glide, not flight), +1% haste, +5% damage to Elementals, and either Skysight (+10% run speed, Horde) or Read Ley Line (double regen, Alliance). The race requires the Skyborne Heroic Pack or higher; everything else in Forever is included with a normal subscription.

Beta status and what is still unknown

  • Beta: September 17 to October 21, 2026. Level cap 20, rising to 30 later. Week one had the Hall of Thanes (13 to 18) and Ruins of Lordaeron (15 to 20) dungeons; more zones, dungeons and the Darkspear Islands battleground open as testing continues. No raids. Nothing carries over.
  • Launch: November 4, 2026 at 3:00 pm Pacific, worldwide. Name reservation October 27 to November 3 for pack buyers. First raids (Barrow Deeps for 10, Hyjal Summit for 20, Onyxia's Lair for 40) on December 9.
  • Realmless: you choose a ruleset, not a server: Normal, PvP or Roleplaying at launch, Hardcore "in winter". The PvP ruleset locks your account to one faction.
  • Unknown as of this writing: dual spec, the riding level and cost, whether any Vanilla profession specialisation changed, the level range of the Shen'dralas zone, and every level-60 balance question. Third-party tier lists exist and disagree with each other; Wowhead's level-20 lists exist but rank a game with no raids and a 20 cap.

How many classes are in WoW Forever?

Nine: Warrior, Paladin, Hunter, Rogue, Priest, Shaman, Mage, Warlock and Druid. There is no new class. All 27 talent trees were reworked, with a new one-point milestone talent at 16 points alongside the ones at 11, 21 and 31.

What are the new race and class combinations in WoW Forever?

Six: Undead Paladin, Dwarf Shaman, Gnome Priest, Human Hunter, Orc Mage and Troll Warlock. Blizzard says more are planned. The new Skyborne race adds Warrior, Hunter, Rogue and Druid on both factions, plus Shaman for the Horde Windshapers and Mage for the Alliance High Order. Tauren still cannot be Paladins.

Is Classic Plus the same as WoW Forever?

Yes. Classic Plus was the community's name for a version of Vanilla WoW that adds new content instead of moving on to expansions. Blizzard announced that product at BlizzCon on September 12, 2026 and named it World of Warcraft: Forever. It launches November 4, 2026 and is described as a permanent third version of WoW alongside Retail and Classic.

Which WoW Forever class is easiest to level?

Icy Veins and Warcraft Tavern both point to Hunter, with Warlock close behind, because a pet does the tanking. Beta players at the level 20 cap report Paladin as the strongest leveler so far thanks to Holy Strike and self-healing. Warrior is the hardest class to level alone, with almost no self-healing.

Does WoW Forever have dual spec?

Not announced. The beta client shows a locked Secondary tab and respecs cost 1 silver during the beta only, which Blizzard says is temporary. Until Blizzard confirms it, plan as if respecs cost gold.
