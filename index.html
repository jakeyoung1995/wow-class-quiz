World of Warcraft: Forever is Blizzard's third permanent version of WoW alongside Retail and Classic, announced at BlizzCon on September 12, 2026. It is the game players called Classic Plus: the original Azeroth with new zones, nine new dungeons, new raids, reworked talent trees for all 27 specs, six new race and class combos, a new race called the Skyborne, and 600 new profession recipes. Blizzard chose the name Forever and describes it as evergreen, with the level cap staying at 60.
WoW Forever launches on November 4, 2026 at 3:00 pm Pacific. The beta runs from September 17 to October 21, 2026, with a level cap of 20 rising to 30. Beta access comes with the Skyborne Epic Pack, the Warcraft Forever Collection, or a Battle.net opt-in. The first raids open on December 9, 2026. Forever is included with a normal WoW subscription; only the Skyborne race requires a paid pack.
